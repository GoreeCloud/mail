Consumer status: adoption-required.
Production eligible from Glaze evidence: no.

The Mail-specific compatibility layer in app/static/style/goreecloud-glaze.less maps current focus, target-size, radius, motion, and accessibility principles onto inherited UI. It is an adoption foundation, not acceptance evidence.

Before Mail can claim Glaze conformance, the exact candidate revision needs rendered, keyboard, focus, assistive-technology, reflow/scaling, reduced-motion, contrast, supported-form-factor, performance, privacy/security, rollback, visual-asset, and human visual acceptance evidence.

First-account preferences now expose keyboard-operable layout choices with selected-state semantics and a labelled shortcut selector. This is a source-level accessibility improvement only. Clean-profile keyboard/focus, screen-reader and rendered Glaze acceptance are still outstanding.
