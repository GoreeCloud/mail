# Upstream Provenance

GoreeCloud Mail began its Fork-to-Native transition from Foundry376/Mailspring.

- Upstream repository: Foundry376/Mailspring
- Imported revision: 6054ff7a2e43b9d8e5ad7931370316e5b35d9a25
- Upstream license: GNU General Public License v3
- Canonical GoreeCloud repository: GoreeCloud/mail

The foundation import is an ancestry-preserving merge. Upstream copyright, license notices, and history remain intact. GoreeCloud Mail is a modified work with independent product direction and is not presented as the upstream Mailspring product.

Upstream updates must be reviewed on a short-lived topic branch. Security, privacy, licensing, dependency, product-service, and feature-preservation impact must be evaluated before synchronization is promoted.
